The conference will take place in the Conference Room, Department of Physics, Himachal Pradesh University, Shimla.

Please refer to the map below for the exact location of the venue. Participants may use Google Maps for directions and travel planning.

Department of Physics, Himachal Pradesh University was established in the year 1969 as part of the Regional Centre of Punjab University, Chandigarh. Later with the establishment of Himachal Pradesh University in 1970, it came into existence as a full-fledged State University Department. Since then it is one of the prestigious Post Graduate Department in the state of Himachal Pradesh offering courses leading to M.Sc. in Physics, and a research programme leading to Ph.D. in Physics. The faculty members are well known and recognized for their research contributions in various fields.
